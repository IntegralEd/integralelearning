Choosing Your Authoring Tool

Storyline or Rise? Sometimes both!

Both tools come with one Articulate 360 license. Picking between them is a per-course design decision, not a purchase decision, and the best courses often use each where it is strongest.

02 · Capabilities

What each tool can do

This is not a versus. Most of what teams need is possible in both tools. The real differences are the learner experience and what your Learning Management System (LMS) gets to see.

Doing vs. seeing

The consultative question is not "can the learner do it." It is "can you see that they did it." In Rise, flashcards and sorting activities engage learners but stay invisible to your LMS. In Storyline, anything can report. When tracking matters, design the interaction in the tool that can tell you about it.

03 · Better together

You don't have to choose

This is the punchline: the two tools are built to work together. Blending them is one option among several, especially when budgets are focused. A fully Rise or fully Storyline course is often exactly right.

Rise as the foundation
Build the course structure, reading, and media in Rise. It publishes quickly and looks great everywhere.
Storyline where it counts
Embed Storyline inside the Rise course for the moments that call for practice, simulation, or scenario choices. The extra design and build time goes exactly where it matters.
Custom code when needed
Both tools accept custom code blocks; our team develops custom code where your project needs it.
